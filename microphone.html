How to check your microphone

Start the microphone, allow access, speak normally and watch the waveform. Record a short sample and use playback to check clarity.

Why is my microphone silent?

Check device mute settings, browser permissions and the selected input device. A low level alone does not prove the hardware is faulty.

Privacy and compatibility

This tool runs in a modern web browser. Where hardware permissions are required, you must grant them explicitly. Device behavior and feature support differ across browsers and operating systems.
