How to test keyboard keys

Choose a keyboard size, click Start test, and press keys. Their positions highlight on the virtual keyboard.

Why do some keys not appear?

Fn, power and operating system shortcuts may not be forwarded to a webpage. Different regional keyboard standards have different physical positions.

Privacy and compatibility

This tool runs in a modern web browser. Where hardware permissions are required, you must grant them explicitly. Device behavior and feature support differ across browsers and operating systems.
