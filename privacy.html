Privacy Policy

DeviceCheck uses browser APIs to help you test devices. Camera previews and microphone recordings stay in your browser and are not transmitted by DeviceCheck. If you download a snapshot or recording, it is saved locally by your browser.

The internet test contacts an external endpoint, which may receive normal HTTP request information such as your IP address. Your hosting provider may also process ordinary web server logs. No analytics or advertising scripts are included in this starter package.

Camera and microphone access require browser permission. You can revoke permissions through your browser settings. We do not collect keyboard-entered text, and keyboard events are used only while testing is activated.

If you later add analytics, ads, contact forms or other services, update this policy accurately before publishing.
