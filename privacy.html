Privacy Policy

DeviceCheck uses browser APIs to help you test devices. Camera previews and microphone recordings stay in your browser and are not transmitted by DeviceCheck. If you download a snapshot or recording, it is saved locally by your browser.

The internet test contacts an external endpoint, which may receive normal HTTP request information such as your IP address. Your hosting provider may also process ordinary web server logs.

DeviceCheck displays advertising provided by Adsterra and its advertising partners. These providers may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices and applicable law. Some advertisements may open a sponsored destination in a new tab or window.

Camera and microphone access require browser permission. You can revoke permissions through your browser settings. We do not collect keyboard-entered text, and keyboard events are used only while testing is activated.
