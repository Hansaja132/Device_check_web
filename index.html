Simple device checks in your browser

DeviceCheck runs most tests locally with browser APIs. Camera and microphone features ask your permission, and test recordings are not uploaded by this website. Some keys, device settings and audio outputs may be restricted by your browser or operating system.

Internet speed estimates require a request to a third-party testing endpoint. No online tester can conclusively diagnose broken hardware; use these tools as a first troubleshooting step.
